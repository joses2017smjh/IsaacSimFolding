# Primary research checked on 19 September 2026

The immediately useful work is completing the current corrected-data evaluations
and observing actual policy failures. The two new adaptation seeds already
exist, so this session adds media rather than duplicating training.

- [Learning to Fold, 25 June 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.27163v1)
  is the directly relevant LeHome solution. Its best-of-N scorer reads candidate
  actions and predicts future outcomes; the local legacy prefix-only score does
  not implement that mechanism. Its selected execution horizons are three
  actions for pants and five for tops. After the current matched baseline,
  short execution horizons are a bounded inference ablation worth testing with
  candidate count held fixed. The paper is an engineering case study with
  limited controlled ablations, not proof that each component will help here.
- [Scaling Bimanual Household Manipulation / XR-2, 3 September 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.03591v1)
  reports expert-data scaling plateaus and gains from corrective on-policy
  demonstrations. The actionable lesson is to retain policy-visited failures
  and collect valid grasp/recovery corrections, especially for weak garment
  classes. Its robot, datasets and reported rates do not transfer directly to
  SO-101 simulation. Failed open-loop replay actions are not expert corrections.
- [FoldNet++, 11 September 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.12433v1)
  studies synthetic T-shirt manipulation data and camera/environment diversity.
  Its wrist-view ablation supports checking both actual wrist observations.
  Focused pre-grasp data and appearance augmentation are useful directions,
  but this does not establish four-class LeHome performance. The
  [official project](https://pku-epic.github.io/FoldNetXX/) still lists code and
  dataset as coming soon; these are not dependencies to assume available.
- [Winning solution source](https://github.com/IliaLarchenko/lehome_solution)
  and [simulation checkpoint](https://huggingface.co/IliaLarchenko/lehome_sim)
  provide an eventual pi0.5 reference. Integrating them requires a separate
  environment and model/data compatibility work. It is not an in-place
  dependency upgrade for the working SmolVLA jobs.

Suggested order after these queued jobs finish: report valid per-class and
Seen/Unseen outcomes; inspect grasp/contact and late-fold failure clips; expand
valid long-pants supervision (the corrected training split has only two
long-pants episodes); test shorter action execution with the same checkpoint;
then implement action-conditioned candidate scoring or actual recorded-trajectory
RECAP training. No new model's success is inferred from validation loss.
